1. Introduction | مقدمة

VIRIFIXO is an independent digital media verification platform designed to help users examine publicly available online video and channel information.

This Privacy Policy explains how VIRIFIXO handles information when users access and use the platform.

VIRIFIXO is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially partnered with YouTube or Google.

2. Information We Process | المعلومات التي نعالجها

VIRIFIXO may process the following information:

  • Publicly available YouTube video and channel information.
  • URLs submitted by users for verification.
  • Basic file metadata when users analyze images or videos locally through their browser.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and improve the service.

3. YouTube API Services | خدمات YouTube API

VIRIFIXO uses YouTube API Services to retrieve and process relevant publicly available YouTube information.

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Uploaded Files and Local Analysis | الملفات والتحليل المحلي

Where local browser-based file analysis is provided, basic file information is processed within the user's browser.

The platform will clearly indicate when information or files are transmitted to external servers.

5. Use of Information | استخدام المعلومات

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • Supporting digital media verification.
  • Examining publicly available video and channel information.
  • Generating structured verification reports.
  • Maintaining and improving platform functionality.
  • Protecting the security and reliability of the service.

6. Data Sharing | مشاركة البيانات

VIRIFIXO does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate the platform or provide requested functionality.

7. Data Security | أمن البيانات

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the platform. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Rights and Choices | حقوق المستخدم وخياراته

Users may choose what publicly available content they submit for examination and may stop using the platform at any time.

9. Third-Party Services | خدمات الأطراف الثالثة

VIRIFIXO may interact with third-party services, including YouTube API Services. Their respective privacy policies and terms apply to their services.

10. Policy Updates | تحديثات السياسة

This Privacy Policy may be updated as VIRIFIXO develops. Updated versions will be published on this page.

11. Contact Information | معلومات التواصل

For privacy-related questions or requests, please use the official project contact channel provided by the VIRIFIXO project owner.
